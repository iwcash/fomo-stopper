# FOMO Stopper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

FOMO Stopper is a Chrome extension by Investor Cognition Lab. It pauses your
order clicks on TradingView and asks three questions before the order goes
through.

## The short version

FOMO Stopper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. Everything
it records stays on your own computer.

## What the extension stores, and where

When the extension pauses an order, it saves one entry to
`chrome.storage.local` in your browser:

- the time of the pause
- the emotion you selected
- your Yes/No answer to "Would I still take this trade 10 minutes from now?"
- the text you typed for "What would prove me wrong?"
- whether you placed or cancelled the order

It also stores two settings (extension on/off and countdown on/off).

This data never leaves your browser. There is no server, account, analytics,
or telemetry. The extension's code contains no network requests. Uninstalling
the extension deletes the data, and "Reset all data" in the popup deletes it
at any time.

## What the extension never reads

FOMO Stopper runs only on `tradingview.com`. It never reads your account
balance, positions, orders, watchlists, or any other page content. The only
thing it inspects is the button you click, to decide whether that click was
an order button.

## Links

The pause screen contains links to the Investor Cognition Lab YouTube
channel. They open in a new tab only if you click them, and they carry no
data from the extension. YouTube's own privacy policy applies once you are on
YouTube.

"Share my stats" draws an image on your computer and downloads it. Nothing is
uploaded or posted.
